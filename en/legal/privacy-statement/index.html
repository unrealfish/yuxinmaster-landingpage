Privacy Policy
Last Updated: September 24, 2025
Yuxin Daozhang (hereinafter referred to as "we") attaches great importance to user privacy and personal information protection. When you use the Yuxin Daozhang platform and services, we may collect and use your related information. Through this Privacy Policy, we explain how we collect, use, and store your personal information, as well as the rights you enjoy.
1. Information We Collect
During your use of Yuxin Daozhang services, we may collect information you provide proactively or information generated during your use of the services as follows:
1.1 Registration Information
When you register an account, you may be required to provide basic information such as your email address, phone number, and username.
When you log in using third-party accounts (e.g., WeChat, Google, Apple, Facebook), we will collect necessary information (such as nickname, profile picture, and email) according to your authorization.
1.2 Device Information
When you use Yuxin Daozhang services, in order to ensure normal service functionality, maintain service stability, improve user experience, and protect your account security, we may collect device information, including device model, operating system, login IP address, and language settings.
1.3 Service Usage Information
When using Yuxin Daozhang services, we collect details of your usage, including interpretation records, click logs, and login logs.
When you use promotional features, we collect details such as invitation records and redemption records.
We do not collect content unrelated to the services.
1.4 Payment Information
When you make top-ups within Yuxin Daozhang, payments are entirely processed by external payment providers (e.g., PayPal or Stripe). We do not store complete payment information, such as credit card numbers. We collect your recharge order information and usage records. This is necessary to provide you with this functionality.
2. How We Use the Information
We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics tools, or any other form of user tracking technology. Only necessary technical data is processed for security and service functionality purposes.
The purposes for which we process the collected data include, but are not limited to:
2.1 Providing Services
To provide you with Yuxin Daozhang services and to maintain and improve these services.
To send you notifications about services or features you may be interested in. You have the right to opt out of such notifications.
2.2 Identity Verification and Authorization
For identity verification, customer service, security measures, fraud detection, archiving, and backup purposes, to ensure the safety of our products and services.
2.3 Maintenance and Security
To detect misuse, errors, or security issues, prevent fraud, and ensure the security of our systems, websites, and services.
2.4 Product Improvement
To analyze data to identify trends, improve products and services, and develop new features.
2.5 Compliance and Legal Requirements
To comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or respond to government or court requests where required.
3. Information Storage
3.1 Storage Location
Yuxin Daozhang operates in multiple countries/regions. Data related to services, support, or payments may be processed by service providers in different jurisdictions.
When transferring personal data, we take appropriate safeguards to ensure secure transmission.
3.2 Retention Period
Server logs are typically retained for a maximum of 7 days for security purposes. In general, we only retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy and as required by applicable laws and regulations. For example:
Email: Whether you registered with your email address or through a third-party account (e.g., WeChat, Google, Apple, Facebook), we retain your email address to ensure your continued use of Yuxin Daozhang services. When you delete your account, we will delete the corresponding information in accordance with applicable law.
We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal information.
If our products or services cease operation, we will notify you via push notifications, announcements, or other legally required means, and will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable timeframe. ("Anonymization" refers to the process of rendering personal information unidentifiable and irreversible. Information processed in this way is no longer considered personal data.)
4. Information Sharing and Transfer
4.1 Sharing
We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Without your explicit consent, we will not share your personal information with any company, organization, or individual, except in the following cases:
(1) With explicit consent: We may share your information with other parties after obtaining your explicit consent.
(2) With affiliates: Your personal information may be shared with our affiliates. Only necessary information will be shared, subject to the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy.
(3) With authorized partners: Certain services may be provided by authorized partners. We may share limited personal information with these partners to provide better customer service and user experience. For example, when you use payment services, we must share order and transaction details with payment institutions. Our partners may not use your information for any other purpose.
4.2 Transfer
We will not transfer your personal information to any company, organization, or individual, except in the following cases:
(1) With explicit consent: We may transfer your information after obtaining your explicit consent.
(2) In the event of mergers, acquisitions, or bankruptcy liquidation, if transfer of personal information is involved, we will require the new entity holding your personal information to be bound by this Privacy Policy; otherwise, we will require them to obtain your consent again.
5. Your Rights
5.1 Access, Update, and Deletion
You have the right to access, update, and delete your personal information, except as otherwise provided by law or regulation.
Personal Information: You can access, update, or delete your personal information by navigating to 【My】-【Personal Information】 in the app.
5.2 Changing Consent
Certain services require essential personal information. For additional data collection, you may grant or withdraw your consent at any time:
(1) You can disable certain permissions such as location, camera, or microphone in your device settings.
(2) You can disable personalized recommendations or notifications in the app’s settings.
Once you withdraw consent, we will no longer process the related data. However, your withdrawal will not affect previous processing based on your authorization.
5.3 Account Deletion
You may request account deletion at any time by contacting our customer service. Please note that account deletion is irreversible. Once deleted, we will erase or anonymize your personal information unless otherwise required by law.
6. How We Protect Personal Data
6.1 We strive to ensure the security of your information against loss, misuse, alteration, unauthorized access, or disclosure.
6.2 We implement various protective measures, including encryption (e.g., SSL), de-identification, and anonymization, to safeguard your data.
6.3 We enhance the security of our client-side software to protect your information, including encrypting certain data locally, analyzing device identifiers, IP addresses, logs, or location data to prevent fraud or unauthorized use.
6.4 In the event of a data breach or other security incident, we will initiate emergency response measures, contain the issue, and fulfill legal reporting and notification obligations.
7. Children’s Information
7.1 Our products and services are intended primarily for adults. Minors should not create accounts without parental or guardian consent.
7.2 Where minors’ information is collected with guardian consent, we will only use or disclose it as permitted by law, with explicit consent, or as necessary to protect minors.
7.3 If we discover that we have collected information from minors without consent, we will delete it as soon as possible.
8. Updates to This Policy
We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. When significant changes occur, we will notify you via login prompts, pop-ups, or other legally compliant means.
9. Contact Us
9.1 If you have any questions, comments, or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@yuxinmaster.site.
9.2 Generally, we will respond within fifteen (15) business days after verifying your identity.
