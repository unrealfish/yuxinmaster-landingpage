Zi Wei Dou Shu

Zi Wei Dou Shu is a classic Eastern star system

Zi Wei Dou Shu ranks first among China's Five Great Divine Arts and is known as the "Number One Divine Art of China." In ancient times it was revered as imperial learning. During the Ming and Qing dynasties, the state established the Qintianjian to study and preserve it, and access was limited to members of the imperial family or officials personally appointed by the emperor.

The star system

Zi Wei Dou Shu contains a rich system of stars. Each star carries its own symbolism and can correspond to personality, situation, relationship and action tendency.

Twelve palaces

The twelve palaces correspond to different life fields, such as self, career, wealth, relationships and movement, allowing star symbolism to land in concrete life contexts.

Star interactions

Zi Wei Dou Shu values the interaction among stars and the changes created by different contexts. Yuxin Master uses these meanings to clarify the key thread of a present question.

A specific, focused question works best. For example, "Is this cooperation suitable to push forward this month?" is clearer than "What will happen to me?"
